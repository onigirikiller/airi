# Safety Rules for Autonomous Minecraft Agent

## Cave / Underground Exploration
- NEVER enter caves without: torch ×4+, food ×2+, sword, pickaxe
- If HP ≤ 6 (30%), retreat and eat before continuing
- Always place torches while exploring to prevent mob spawns

## Mining Tier Rules
- Stone/cobblestone: requires wooden pickaxe minimum
- Iron ore: requires stone pickaxe minimum
- Diamond/gold/redstone ore: requires iron pickaxe minimum
- Obsidian: requires diamond pickaxe

## Combat
- Do NOT fight with bare hands
- If HP ≤ 4 (20%), disengage and heal
- Always have food available for regeneration during combat

## Crafting Dependencies
- 3×3 recipes require a crafting table nearby or in inventory
- Smelting requires a furnace + fuel (coal/wood/planks)
- Always craft tools in upgrade order: wood → stone → iron → diamond

## Night Safety
- If no bed and nighttime, seek shelter or light area with torches
- Avoid open exploration at night without armor + sword

## Food
- Keep at least 2 food items at all times
- If hunger bar is below 6, prioritize finding/cooking food
